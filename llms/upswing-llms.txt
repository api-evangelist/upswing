# Upswing

> Upswing Financial Technologies is a Bengaluru/Mumbai-based open finance-as-a-service platform. Its API platform lets consumer companies embed financial products (deposits, lending) from regulated Indian banks, while Upswing manages the bank integrations and compliance. Backed by QED Investors and Quona Capital. Upswing publishes no public developer portal, API documentation, or machine-readable specs; its APIs are available to partners under commercial agreements.
